Guide

Welcome to Friendz

  1. Create your profile and add a picture.
  2. Open Friends to find people and send requests.
  3. Private chats unlock after the request is accepted.
  4. Use Public Chat to talk to everyone.
  5. Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music link in Dashboard to share what you're watching.
  6. Enter the giveaway before the five-day timer expires.

Watching activity is shared using the link you provide; a website cannot automatically detect everything you watch in other apps.
