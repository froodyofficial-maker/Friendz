Guide

Welcome to Friendz

  1. Complete your setup and add a profile picture.
  2. Open Friends to find group members and start a direct chat.
  3. Use Public Chat to message everyone in the group.
  4. Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music link in Dashboard to share what you're watching.
  5. Enter the giveaway before its five-day timer expires.

Watching activity is shared by the user: websites cannot automatically see what you watch in another tab or app. Automatic browser-wide detection would require an optional browser extension.
